# Accessibility

Use LarisVMS with a keyboard, a screen reader, or your own display settings.

## Keyboard

Every page starts with a **Skip to main content** link, which appears when you press Tab.

| Where | Keys |
|---|---|
| Live and view tiles | Tab reaches a tile's controls, which appear as you reach them |
| PTZ buttons | Hold Enter or Space to move, and release to stop |
| Timeline | Left and Right arrows nudge the time, Page Up and Page Down jump, and + and - zoom |
| Current time on Playback | Enter opens a field for typing an exact time |
| Zones, privacy masks, speed traps, and redaction | Arrow keys move a cursor (Shift for bigger steps), Space clicks, Enter finishes a shape, and Backspace removes the last point |
| Dashboard and view layouts | Each item has buttons to move and resize it |
| Map | Press Enter on a camera to place it mid-map, then use the move buttons |

To move a point on a shape, press Space on it, move the cursor, and press Space again. A mouse works the same way: click a point, then click where it should go.

To draw a redaction rectangle without dragging, click one corner, then the opposite corner.

## Screen readers

Controls, form fields, and status messages are labeled for screen readers, and errors are announced when a page loads. A manual recording starting or stopping on a tile in your view is announced too.

Exact values on the Activity page are available as a table: turn on the table option above the chart.

Video itself is visual. Each video is named after its camera, but what the picture shows can't be described automatically.

## Seeing the timeline without color

Timeline stretches differ by pattern as well as color:

- **Empty:** no footage.
- **Solid:** footage was recorded.
- **Striped:** footage with motion or an event.

## Display settings

- **Theme:** LarisVMS follows your device's light or dark setting, or use the 🌙 button at the top of the page.
- **Text size:** a larger default font size in your browser applies here, and browser zoom works on every page.
- **Reduced motion:** animations are turned off if your device asks for less motion.
- **High contrast:** Windows contrast themes are supported.

Under **Settings → Branding**, LarisVMS warns if a chosen color is hard to read. Custom colors apply to everyone, so choose them with care.

## Pausing updates

Use **Pause updates** on the Dashboard to stop widgets changing while you read them. Live video keeps playing, because it's what the page is for.

## Limitations

- **Captions and audio description:** cameras don't record captions or audio description, so the player has no controls for them.
- **Drawing without sight:** shapes on a camera picture can be drawn from the keyboard, but placing them needs to be done by eye.
- **Tile timelines:** the small timeline on a live tile zooms with the mouse wheel, a pinch, or the + and - keys. The full timeline on Playback also has zoom buttons.
